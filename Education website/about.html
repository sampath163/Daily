About Campus :

The College is situated in an eco-friendly area of 180 acres with thick greenery at Surampalem, Gandepalli Mandal, East Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh. The College is 15 KM away from Samalkot Railway Station on Howrah-Chennai Railway line in South Central Railway. The College is 35 Km away from Kakinada and Rajahmundry on ADB Road.

The campus is centrally located between Kakinada and Rajahmundry. It is situated in a eco-friendly area with thick greenery at Surampalem, Gandepalli Mandal, East Godavari District, AP. ACET offers various under graduate and post graduate courses in engineering, science and management and has state of laboratories and well stocked library and one of the best computing facilities. With an ideal teacher-taught ratio we strive for academic excellence through personalized attention. Since its inception ACET has achieved national standing in terms of academic performance, co-curricular and extra curricular activities. Known for its creative dynamism and flexibility the college offers varied programs blending skill development and value orientation to shape the carreer of students.
