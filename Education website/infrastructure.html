Infrastructure

  1. Library

    The central library is well equipped with all the World class facilities and latest technology to disseminate information, Render speedy, efficient and excellent services to the user community. In view of the growing importance of automation the central library purchased library software to provide effective services to the users in Library operations like Acquisition, Cataloguing, Circulation and Serial Control have been automated using the ECAP software. All the books are bar-coded, Log-in automated with gate scanners and Library works under CC Camera Surveillance.

  2. Hostel

    It doesn't get better than this, the hostel facilities which we provide are the need of the hour for students. With nutritious food, good bedding, spacious rooms our students would get to spend a comfortable and happy time.

      Some of the Key Features

    • Nutritious North Indian & South Indian dishes
    • 24/7 academic guidance by resident faculty
    • Well furnished hostel rooms with attached bathrooms

    • Gym

    Around 220 buses help our students to commute and commute with ease therefore reducing the burden of travel through other transport facilities. With a fleet of 310 buses, a transport facility is provided to every corner of the district for the students and staff at regular intervals, so that they can reach the institutions well in time and get back to their domiciles with a safe journey as per the schedule.

  1. Karting Championship

    Kart racing or Karting is a variant of open-wheel motor-sport with small, open, four-wheeled vehicles called karts, go-karts, or gearbox/shifter karts depending on the design. They are usually raced on scaled-down circuits.Karts vary widely in speed (known as recreational go-karts ) intended for limited to lower speeds. and some (known as Superkarts) can reach speeds exceeding 260 kilometres per hour (160 mph), From age 5 to 75, racing go karts have become popular all over the world with people looking for an exciting way of having fun. In fact, many amusement parks have added rental racing go karts (called concession karts) that use detuned 4-stroke go-cart engines for a milder experience.
