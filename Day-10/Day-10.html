Grilled-Chicken.

$10

Grilled chicken is a popular, lean dish made by cooking marinated or seasoned chicken over direct or indirect heat on a grill. To prevent it from drying out, pound the meat to an even thickness, cook over medium-high heat for 5 to 7 minutes per side, and let it rest until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.

Chole-Bhature.

Chole Bhature is a famous, rich North Indian dish featuring spicy, tangy chickpea curry (chole) paired with soft, fluffy deep-fried leavened bread (bhature).

Vada-Pav.

$3

Make famous Mumbai-style Vada Pav at home by stuffing a spicy, deep-fried potato fritter (batata vada) inside a soft bread roll (pav) with green and dry garlic chutneys. This iconic Indian street food is hot, spicy, and full of rich flavors.
